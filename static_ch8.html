WTO Agricultural Subsidy Boxes Repeated SSC Concept

WTO categorizes domestic agricultural support using traffic light analogies based on trade-distortion risk:

Box Category Trade Impact Conditions & Real-World Examples
Green Box Non-distorting or minimal distortion. Government funding for agricultural R&D, pest control, disaster relief, decoupled income support. Fully permitted.
Blue Box Production-limiting subsidies. Direct government payments linked to fixed areas and yields, requiring farmers to limit production. Allowed with caps.
Amber Box Directly distorts international trade. Direct price supports, inputs subsidies (power/fertilizer), and Minimum Support Price (MSP). Subject to strict reduction commitments.
AI Addition / High PYQ Factor Indian MSP & FRP Institutional Mechanics
  • CACP: The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (established 1965) recommends MSPs for 22 mandated crops (plus FRP for Sugarcane).
  • Approval Authority: The final decision on MSP is taken by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA), chaired by the Prime Minister.
  • Sugarcane: Sugarcane is covered under Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP), not MSP.

AI Addition / History PYQ Link Teacher's Unanswered Question in Lecture

During the lecture, the teacher asks: "Which commission recommended the separation of Burma (Myanmar) from India?"
Answer: The Simon Commission (1930) recommended the separation of Burma from British India, which was subsequently enacted under the Government of India Act, 1935 (formally separated in 1937).

11. Chabahar Port (Iran)

Gateway to Afghanistan & Central Asia
Strategic Importance & Operational Rights High Priority / Direct PYQ
  • Geographic Location: Sistan-Baluchistan Province, southeastern Iran, on the Gulf of Oman.
  • Strategic Bypass: Allows India direct trade access to Afghanistan and Central Asia, completely bypassing Pakistan.
  • Two Main Terminals: Shahid Beheshti and Shahid Kalantari.
  • 10-Year Long-Term Contract: Signed on 13 May 2024 for operations at the Shahid Beheshti Terminal.
  • Indian Operating Agency: India Ports Global Limited (IPGL).
  • 1st Wheat Consignment to Afghanistan: Dispatched through Chabahar in October 2017 (via Zaranj-Delaram highway link).
  • Regional Rival Port: Counterbalances China-operated Gwadar Port in Pakistan.

13. IMEC (India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor)

G20 Delhi Initiative & Counter to BRI
Core Architecture & Participant Nations 100% Guaranteed Question Area
  • Announcement: Unveiled via an MoU on 9 September 2023 during the G20 Leaders Summit in New Delhi.
  • Geopolitical Purpose: A US-backed sustainable alternative countering China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI).
  • Key Signatories: India, USA, Saudi Arabia, UAE, European Union, France, Germany, Italy.
  • Two Discrete Route Corridors:
    • East Corridor: Connecting ports of West India to the Arabian Gulf (UAE/Saudi Arabia).
    • Northern Corridor: Connecting the Arabian Gulf to European ports via rail and Mediterranean ports.
  • Efficiency Projections: Reduces logistics costs by ~30% and slashes transit time by ~40%.
  • Scope of Infrastructure: Rail networks, ship-to-rail transit, undersea electricity links, optical fiber cables, and clean hydrogen pipelines.

16. High-Yield Lecture MCQ Revision Drill

Click Questions to Reveal Answers & Analytical Traps
1. Which company was awarded the 10-year contract to operate the Shahid Beheshti terminal at Chabahar Port? Reveal ↓
Answer: India Ports Global Limited (IPGL).
Exam Trap: Do not mark Adani Ports or Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT). Contract signed on 13 May 2024.
2. What two broad geographical regions does the IMEC initiative aim to connect through the Middle East? Reveal ↓
Answer: Asia (India) and Europe.
Breakdown: Eastern Corridor links India to the Arabian Gulf; Northern Corridor links the Gulf to Europe.
3. What is the difference between Amber Box, Blue Box, and Green Box subsidies under WTO? Reveal ↓
Answer:
• Green Box: Minimal or no distortion (allowed without limits, e.g., R&D).
• Blue Box: Subsidies that require farmers to limit production.
• Amber Box: Distorts trade (e.g., MSP, input subsidies; strictly capped).
4. Which two countries joined BIMSTEC in February 2004 as its latest members? Reveal ↓
Answer: Nepal and Bhutan.
Founding: Started in 1997 as BIST-EC; Myanmar joined in late 1997; Nepal & Bhutan joined in 2004.
5. Which port was built with Chinese assistance in Sri Lanka and leased to China for 99 years? Reveal ↓
Answer: Hambantota Port.
Geopolitical Concept: Key node of China's "String of Pearls" strategy in the Indian Ocean.
6. Under the rationalized BIMSTEC structure, which Lead Pillar is headed by India? Reveal ↓
Answer: Security (comprising Counter-Terrorism, Transnational Crime, Disaster Management, and Energy).
