1. The Constitutional Framework (Parts & Articles)

The Three Branches of Center-State Relations [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]

The Constitution primarily divides relations between the Union and the States into three distinct dimensions spanning across Part XI and Part XII:

Relation Dimension Part Article Span Exam Priority
Legislative Relations Part XI (11) Art. 245 – 255 [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]
Administrative Relations Part XI (11) Art. 256 – 263 [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]
Financial Relations Part XII (12) Art. 268 – 293 [STANDARD EXAM FACT]
Teacher's Exact Cue: Emphasized repeatedly in lecture. Memorize the exact boundaries: Part 11 splits at 255/256. Part 12 picks up at 268.
Residuary Powers of Legislation [STANDARD EXAM FACT]

The power to legislate on residuary subjects (subjects not enumerated in Union, State, or Concurrent Lists) rests exclusively with the Parliament.

Adjacent Constitutional Provision:
• Article 248: Exclusive power of Parliament with respect to Residuary Powers.
• Entry 97 (Union List): Any other matter not enumerated in List II or List III.
• Source of borrowing: Canada (vesting residuary powers in the Center). [EXTRA SSC PYQ / FACT]

2. Out-of-the-Box Reorganisation Act PYQ

Punjab Reorganisation Act [STANDARD EXAM FACT]

Year of Passage: 1966

Passed following the recommendations of the Shah Commission. Resulted in the trifurcation into the Hindi-speaking state of Haryana, Punjabi-speaking state of Punjab, and the Union Territory of Chandigarh (plus transfer of hilly regions to Himachal Pradesh).

Teacher's Exact Cue: Flagged as an "out-of-the-box" question that frequently catches candidates off guard in SSC tier-1/tier-2.

3. Inter-State Dispute Mechanisms & Coordination

Inter-State River Water Disputes [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]

Adjudication of disputes relating to waters of inter-state rivers or river valleys:

  • Empowered Authority: Parliament may by law provide for adjudication.
  • Judicial Exclusion: Parliament can exclude the Supreme Court and all other courts from having jurisdiction over such disputes.
[EXTRA SSC PYQ / FACT] Article 262: The exact constitutional article governing Inter-State Water Disputes. Parliament enacted the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 under this power.
Inter-State Council [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]

Appointing Authority: The President of India has the sole constitutional authority to establish and appoint an Inter-State Council to promote Center-State coordination.

[EXTRA SSC PYQ / FACT]
• Article: Article 263.
• Commission: Established on the recommendations of the Sarkaria Commission (1987-88); officially set up by Presidential Order in 1990.
• Chairman: Prime Minister of India.

4. Parmar Sir's Elimination Technique (Homework Question)

Bracket Elimination Strategy for SSC Options [HIGH YIELD / REPEATED]

In the lecture, Parmar Sir introduced the final homework question and stressed that knowing the article brackets alone eliminates at least two distractors instantly.

The Bracket Filter Logic:
1. If a question is about law-making / territories / subject distribution, the answer MUST fall in Art. 245–255.
2. If a question is about executive directions / coordination / disputes, the answer MUST fall in Art. 256–263.
3. If a question is about grants / taxes / finance / borrowing, eliminate Part XI immediately and look only at Art. 268–293.
