2. BNS (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023)

Key Structural Evolutions Standard Concept

The BNS was designed to modernize and decolonize Indian penal law, excising antiquated Victorian notions while codifying contemporary offenses.

358
Total Sections (Reduced from 511 in IPC)
20
Total Chapters
6
Petty Crimes with Community Service
  • Decriminalized / Scrapped:
    • Adultery: Entirely removed (previously struck down in Joseph Shine v. Union of India).
    • IPC Section 377: Same-sex consensual intercourse fully decriminalized (pursuant to Navtej Singh Johar case).
  • Redefining Sedition (IPC 124A Scrapped): The colonial word "Sedition" (Rajdroh) is eliminated. It is replaced under Section 152 with acts "Endangering Sovereignty, Unity, and Integrity of India" (Deshdroh).
  • New Punishment Category — Community Service: Introduced for the first time in Indian criminal law under Section 4 (and Sec. 23 definition context) for 6 petty/minor offenses (e.g., small theft, non-injurious public misconduct, simple defamation) to prevent prison congestion and foster rehabilitation.

3. BNS: Chapter V (Crimes Against Women & Children)

Consolidation in One Dedicated Chapter High-Yield Elimination Anchor

Under the IPC, offenses against women and children were scattered across various chapters. The BNS consolidates them systematically into Chapter V (Sections 63 to 99).

Elimination Master Key: If SSC asks about any section pertaining to rape, gang rape, voyeurism, stalking, dowry cruelty, or deceitful marriage promises, and the options feature numbers outside 63–99, eliminate them immediately!
Section Range Specific Provision Crucial Notes
Section 63 – 64 Definition and Punishment for Rape Consolidated foundational sections.
Section 69 Deceitful promise to marry / false identity Penalizes sexual relations procured by fraud or deceitful marriage promises.
Section 70(2) Gang Rape of a Minor Death Penalty or Life Imprisonment for gang rape of a girl below 18 years (expanded from age 12 in earlier framework).
Section 77 Voyeurism Watching or capturing images of a woman in private acts.
Section 78 Stalking Following, contacting, or monitoring a woman electronically or physically.
Section 84 Cruelty by Husband or Relatives Corresponds to former IPC 498A.
Section 99 Terminal Section of Chapter V Marks the outer boundary for offenses against women/children.
⚡ SSC Value-Add: Courtroom Representation Rule (Sec. 21 BNSS) For trials of offenses under Sections 64 to 71 of BNS (crimes of sexual nature against women), the proceedings shall, as far as practicable, be presided over by a female judge/magistrate.

5. BNSS (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023)

Strict Timelines & Victim-Centric Procedures Highest Frequency in SSC

The BNSS prioritizes speedy trials, limits procedural adjournment culture, and mandates rigorous timelines at every stage of investigation.

Revolutionary BNSS Procedural Changes Teacher Stressed

  • Police Custody Remand in Staggered Installments (Section 187):
    • Under CrPC, 15 days of police custody could only be sought continuously in the first 15 days of arrest.
    • Under BNSS Section 187, the 15-day police custody can now be authorized in whole or in parts/staggered installments throughout the entire 40 to 60 days (for <10 yr jail crimes) or 60 to 90 days remand period.
  • Trial in Absentia (Proclaimed Offenders): If a proclaimed offender accused of a crime punishable by 10 years or more, life imprisonment, or death absconds to evade trial and cannot be produced, the court can proceed with the trial and pronounce judgment in their absence!
  • Mandatory Forensic Science Investigation (Section 176): For all offenses punishable with imprisonment of 7 years or more, forensic experts must mandatorily visit the crime scene and collect evidence with videography.
  • Zero FIR & e-FIR (Section 173):
    • Zero FIR: FIR can be registered at any police station regardless of territorial jurisdiction, transferred later.
    • e-FIR: FIR registered electronically from home; must be formally signed within 3 days.
    • Tracked via CCTNS (Crime and Criminal Tracking Network and Systems).
  • First-Time Undertrial Prisoner Relief (Section 479): A first-time offender (never convicted previously) shall be released on personal bond/bail after undergoing detention for 1/3rd of the maximum sentence prescribed (standard rule for regular undertrials remains 1/2). Does NOT apply to death or life imprisonment offenses.
  • Specimen Collection Expansion: Investigating officers can now take Voice Samples alongside handwriting specimens and finger impressions.
  • Summary Trials Limit: Summary trials (speedy trials for non-serious offenses) carry a maximum imprisonment term limit of 3 months.

7. BSA (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023)

Evidence & Admissibility Framework Standard Concept

The BSA modernizes judicial evidence by according digital, electronic, and technological records the exact same legal status as traditional paper documents.

170
Total Sections
12
Total Chapters
4
Total Parts
Concept BSA Section Rule of Law & Admissibility
Definition of Document Section 2 Now includes electronic/digital records: server logs, emails, smartphones, cloud data, location data, voicemails, messages.
Admissibility of Electronic Records Section 63 Electronic records have identical legal status as physical documents (replaces old Sec. 65B of IEA). Requires validation certificate from an independent tech expert.
Confession to Police Officer Inadmissible
Teacher Stressed		 Any confession given directly to a police officer is completely inadmissible in court. Exception: Only that specific portion that leads to the physical recovery of an object (e.g., hidden gun, bullets) is admissible.
Dying Declaration Section 26 Statements made by a person as to the cause of their death are admissible.
Joint Trial Confession Joint Trial Rule If multiple accused face a joint trial for the same offense, a proven confession by one implicating another is treated as a confession against both.
Doctrine of Estoppel Section 121 A person who induces another to believe and act on a representation cannot later renege on that statement.

10. Parmar's NETO & Question Elimination Rules

Rules of Thumb for SSC CGL General Studies Exam-Hall Hacks

  • The "Lengthy / Explanatory" Heuristic (NETO Rule): When facing 4 options in complex polity/law questions, the correct choice is very often the most comprehensive, grammatically nuanced option featuring commas, qualifiers, or bracketed conditions (e.g., "as far as practicable, presided over by a woman" or "imprisonment up to two years, or fine, or both, or community service"). Examiners write longer text when strictly copying statutory legal clauses to play it safe.
  • Extreme Option Elimination: Eliminate choices demanding mandatory AI automation, immediate death penalty for minor offenses, or complete scrapping of basic constitutional bail rights.
  • Deemed Sanction Rule: If the government fails to decide on prosecution sanction against a public servant/officer within 120 days (or general statutory limit), it is legally treated as Deemed Permission / Deemed Sanction.
  • Cognizable vs Non-Cognizable Shortcut:
    • Cognizable: Serious offenses → Police arrest WITHOUT a warrant.
    • Non-Cognizable: Minor offenses → Police REQUIRE a warrant from a Magistrate.
    • Non-Compoundable: Serious crimes (murder, rape) → No compromise/settlement permitted out of court.
